## 4. THE TWO MAIN BETS

### BET 1 — Own the switching moment (conquest machine)

This is the one tactic every 2026 winner visibly ran: Decodo, IPRoyal and ProxyScrape built entire `/alternatives/` URL directories, NodeMaven/DataImpulse/Proxy-Seller shipped "NetNut shutdown explained" pages within days. [Decodo](https://decodo.com/best/netnut-alternatives), [IPRoyal](https://iproyal.com/proxy-alternatives/netnut/), [Hex](https://hexproxies.com/migrations/migrate-from-netnut)

What Ziny builds (Claude Code can scaffold it in days, cash cost zero):

1. **`/alternatives/{brand}` + `/migrate-from/{brand}` for ~25 brands.** Dead ones (NetNut, 922, Luna, PIA S5, IP2World, PYProxy, ABC) and live ones whose customers are angriest right now (ranked by the competitor track):
   - Proxy-Cheap: Trustpilot 2.8 / 746, "fraud scores 80–89%", pre-banned static IPs.
   - Thordata: Trustpilot rating *suspended* for guideline breach, "free trial, still charged".
   - 9Proxy: multi-week outages Aug–Sep 2026 while still selling.
   - IPRoyal: 3.6 / 2,640, largest unhappy base, "DC IPs sold as resi", refunds denied.
   - Oxylabs / Bright Data KYC refugees: "flagged right after signing up", "KYC impossible".
   Each page needs the technical walkthrough buyers actually use: endpoint mapping, sticky-session syntax, auth format, country codes. Not a listicle.
2. **Balance-match offer.** Nobody found doing a literal "upload your old invoice, we credit up to X GB". It's just credits. Cheapest conquest offer in the industry and an open lane.
3. **Remove every switching friction the giants added**: no KYC before purchase, crypto accepted, true PAYG with a $5–10 minimum, **non-expiring traffic** (the #1 praise point for IPRoyal/DataImpulse), published refund policy honoured within 24h over chat. These are pricing/policy changes, not builds, and they are exactly what the Trustpilot complaints say is missing.
4. **Answer the complaints where they vent**: Reddit, BHW, Telegram threads of dead/unstable brands. Claude drafts, Val posts. Keep G2 review velocity up so Ziny auto-appears in G2's "Top 10 X alternatives" pages, which rank #1 for "IPIDEA alternative".

Why this is Bet 1: zero cash, highest intent traffic in the industry (people who already decided to leave), and it converts the segment the big players are rejecting.

### BET 2 — Get into the point of purchase (antidetect stores, aggregators, resellers)

Proxy purchase intent peaks at profile-creation inside antidetect browsers, and inside scraping aggregators. Competitors already listed there are small (WTFProxy, Floppydata, PrivateProxy, Froxy), so Ziny can get in.

1. **In-app proxy stores and partner centers**: AdsPower Partner Center ([adspower.com/partner-plan](https://www.adspower.com/partner-plan), apply, reply in 3 business days, no fee), Octo Browser Proxy Shop + partner page, Hidemyacc Proxy Store (100+ partner discount codes), Dolphin Anty integrated providers, GoLogin `/proxies/` directory, Multilogin blog listing. Plus mutual partner-page swaps with Kameleo, Undetectable, MoreLogin, Linken Sphere, and the ones Ziny already has guides for (GeeLark, VMLogin, Xlogin, OmniLogin, BitBrowser). Application is a form + a discount code.
2. **ScrapeOps Residential Proxy Aggregator**: lists Froxy, DataImpulse, HydraProxy, WTFProxy, "adding providers every week". Email them an API key + pricing. [scrapeops.io](https://scrapeops.io/residential-mobile-proxy-aggregator/)
3. **Wholesale / reseller tier.** Telegram/Discord/Whop/BHW micro-sellers with 50–2,000 customers lost their upstream twice this year. Offer sub-user API + prepaid deposits + white-label. Ziny's Whop (416 members) and Laravel dashboard are the starting assets. Margin test first: wholesale has to land at or under ~$1.2–1.5/GB to work given Ziny is itself a reseller.
4. **Product fit for this channel: dedicated (non-rotating) mobile lines per country.** The one under-served pocket with a recurring ticket ($50–130/line/mo). Lead with US/UK/CA + 2 emerging GEOs via a SIM-farm upstream. Reprice rotating mobile to ≤$1.50/GB or stop leading with it (Rayobyte is at $0.50).

Why this is Bet 2: it's where Ziny's existing customers (agencies, multi-accounters, cloaker users) already shop, the giants just banned that use case, and listing is nearly free.

### ENABLER — the funnel that feeds both bets

- **No-card free tier on dashboard.ziny.io** (100–500 MB resi + free proxy checker / IP page). Webshare's free tier is the only traffic engine in the industry with hard numbers (2.4–3.8M visits/mo). "Free trial on request" is a dead end; the 2026 swarm of affiliate review sites (proxyfacts, proxyradar, proxyon, proxylook, proxyverdict, caproxy, catproxies…) and coupon pages will link to any provider with a payout + a trial URL.
- **Affiliate 50% recurring** (Decodo: 50% recurring, 60-day cookie) and push it to affmaven/afftank/affiliate.watch "best proxy affiliate programs" lists.
- **Reviews**: open Trustpilot, claim G2 fully, automate post-purchase invites. Compounding and cheap.
- **Fix the claim gap**: either get the sourcing story straight with the upstream and publish it plainly, or drop "30M+ verified consented IPs" and "4.9 on SourceForge". A weekly public fraud-score test of Ziny vs 6 competitors (IPQS/Scamalytics) is the proof buyers want and nobody small publishes.

## 6. Stop / avoid

- Investing in torrent/porn/streaming unblock SEO (keep URLs for equity).
- DePIN/airdrop content (Grass penalises proxies now).
- Building an unlocker, scrape API, or "AI agent" product. "Proxy for AI agents" as a bet: DoubleData says the demand "did not hold up" outside enterprise.
- SERP/rank-tracker white-label, ticket bots, TG/WhatsApp bulk-sender buyers.
- Brand-bidding Google Ads (no evidence it works in proxies, trademark limits), Product Hunt/HN (no proxy brand found with traction), paid BHW threads until the free tier exists.
- Unverifiable homepage claims.
